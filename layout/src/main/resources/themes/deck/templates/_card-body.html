{# Deck theme: a slide is a fixed skeleton, so blocks are PLACED, not looped. Touching card.slots at all switches on LayoutEngine's placement invariant — every top-level block consumed, every require() matched, or the build fails with SlotPlacementException naming the card and the block. That is the whole reason a deck template is slot-based: an unplaced region on a slide is a bug, and nobody proofreads sixty slides the way they proofread a chapter. The region classes are also the contract with the pptx renderer: it reads .slide-body / .slide-aside as the card's slot signature and picks a matching PowerPoint slideLayout from it. Renaming them here changes that mapping. Authoring shape — the body is the text BEFORE any heading, so a slide needs no ceremonial "## Body": --- title: What we get for free --- - first bullet - second bullet ## Aside Optional second column. ## Notes Speaker notes. Never rendered; lifted into the pptx notes pane. rest() is the safety net, not the notes mechanism. Leftovers go to the notes rather than onto the slide, marked notes--unplaced so the renderer can warn about them — a heading typo that quietly relocated a visible region is worse than a build error, because the slide still looks finished.
